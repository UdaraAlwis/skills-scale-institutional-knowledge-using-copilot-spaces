# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains the core project management guidance for OctoAcme. Together, these documents define how the team initiates work, plans delivery, tracks execution, manages risks and communication, releases changes, and improves its processes over time.

## Process summary

OctoAcme manages work through a lifecycle of initiation, planning, iterative execution, release, and continuous improvement. Initiation validates the business need, measurable outcomes, stakeholders, timeline, and initial risks before a go/no-go decision. Approved initiatives are planned as prioritized, estimated work with acceptance criteria, milestones, a Definition of Done, and documented dependencies. During execution, teams track progress on a project board, surface blockers, and use regular check-ins and reviews to keep delivery on course.

The process relies on clear ownership: Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; developers implement and test the work; QA validates quality and acceptance; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Communication includes PM–Product Manager alignment, team standups, weekly delivery and risk reviews, demos, stakeholder updates, and defined escalation paths. Quality is supported by unit and integration tests as appropriate, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, and CI checks for tests, linting, and security scanning.

Before release, teams verify acceptance criteria and CI results, prepare release notes and rollback or mitigation plans, and plan smoke tests. They deploy and perform post-deployment checks, then communicate the release to stakeholders. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents capture lessons and turn them into a small number of actionable improvements with owners and due dates.
