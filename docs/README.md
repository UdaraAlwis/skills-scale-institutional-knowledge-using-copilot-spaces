# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains the core project management guidance for OctoAcme. Together, these documents define how the team initiates work, plans delivery, tracks execution, manages risks and communication, releases value safely, and continuously improves how projects run.

## Process summary

OctoAcme manages work through a simple lifecycle: initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. During initiation, the team validates the business problem, stakeholders, success metrics, timeline, and resource needs before deciding whether to proceed. Once approved, work is broken into a prioritized backlog with clear acceptance criteria, milestones, a Definition of Done, and an initial risk and dependency list. During execution, teams use a project board, standups, and review checkpoints to move work forward in small, testable increments. Releases require quality gates such as automated tests, CI checks, security scans, smoke testing, and rollback readiness. After major milestones or incidents, retrospectives capture lessons learned and convert them into action items with owners and due dates.

The processes also define clear responsibilities across key roles. Project Managers coordinate delivery, risks, and communications; Product Managers set outcomes and priorities; developers implement and validate the work; QA ensures acceptance and quality; and stakeholders provide input, approvals, and strategic alignment. Communication is structured around regular standups, weekly syncs, milestone reviews, stakeholder updates, and escalation paths for blockers or incidents. This creates a repeatable rhythm that keeps work visible, aligned, and accountable across the team.
